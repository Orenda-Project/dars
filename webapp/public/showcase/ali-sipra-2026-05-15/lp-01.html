SLO

🎯 Students will be able to read the story aloud with correct pronunciation and understand the main idea of the text. (Understand)

Hook

Ask students if they have ever visited a big market or grocery shop with their parents.

Ask: "What do you do if you cannot see your mother or father in a big shop?"

Tell the students that today we will read a story about a little girl named Pinky who gets lost in a big store.

Explanation

Write these difficult words on the board and tell their meanings: Adventure (an exciting activity), Grocery (a shop that sells food), Butterfly (an insect with colorful wings), Fluttery (moving quickly like a bird).

Read the story "Pinky's Store Adventure!" aloud slowly with good expressions, pointing to the words while students look at their books.

Ask: Who is Pinky talking to in the story?

Ask: What did Pinky see in the store that made her stop?

Tell the students the main idea of the story is that we should stay in one place if we get lost, and we must learn our parents' phone number and address.

Practice

Ask students to open their English books to page 111.

Pick a paragraph randomly from the text and call one student to stand up and read it independently.

Repeat this by choosing different paragraphs and different students to read aloud for practice.

Help students who are facing difficulty by reading the hard words first and asking them to repeat after you.

Conclusion

Ask the following True/False questions to review the lesson:

  • Q1: True or False: Pinky got lost in a park.
  • Answer 1: False
  • Q2: True or False: Abbu told Pinky to stay in one place if she gets lost.
  • Answer 2: True
