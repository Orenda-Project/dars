1. Objectives (2 Minutes)

Students will be able to locate specific information to answer comprehension questions using the Keyword Hunting strategy. [Apply]

2. Resources & Teacher Support 💡

  • Textbook Page 49
  • Whiteboard and markers (preferably red and blue)
  • Visual Anchor: A drawing on the board of a large magnifying glass hovering over the word "Sir Riaz" to represent the Keyword Hunting strategy.

Teacher Note: When teaching Keyword Hunting, remind students to scan for capital letters (names) or unique words instead of reading every single word again. When you lose your favorite toy at home, do you look in every single room, or do you go straight to the places you usually play?

3. Introduction 🚀 (5 Minutes)

Good morning, my wonderful Grade 5 students! I am so excited to see you all today.

When you need to find a specific video on YouTube or a game on a phone, what exactly do you type in the search bar to find it quickly?

Word of the Day: Cherish (to love, protect, and care for something deeply).

Today, we are going to learn how to search our reading passages just like a search engine, using a strategy called Keyword Hunting!

4. Explanation & Teaching 🧠 (10 Minutes)

Visual Aid: The teacher writes a sample sentence on the board: "Ahmed went to the park to cherish the sunny weather." The teacher visually circles the word "Ahmed" in red and "park" in blue.

Watch me closely. If my question is 'Where did Ahmed go?', I do not need to read the whole page again. I pick a keyword from the question. The keyword is 'Ahmed' and 'where'. I will scan the text with my finger until I spot 'Ahmed'. Ah, here it is! Then I read the rest of the sentence to find the place. He went to the park!

CFU:

  • Q1: If the question asks, 'What did Sir Riaz say?', what is our keyword to hunt for?
  • Answer 1: Sir Riaz.
  • Q2: If the question asks, 'Why did Pinky go to the market?', what is our keyword?
  • Answer 2: Pinky or market.
  • Q3: Do we read the whole paragraph slowly when we Keyword Hunt, or do we scan quickly with our eyes?
  • Answer 3: We scan quickly with our eyes.

5. Practice ✍️ (22 Minutes)

It is time to use your detective skills to conquer the textbook!

Teacher's Decision Point: The teacher may choose one or more of the following options based on class readiness:

Option 1: Pair students up (e.g., Ali with Fatima, Ahmed with Zainab). Have them complete Activity 2 (Fill in the Missing Letter) together, then use Keyword Hunting to underline the answers for Activity 3 in their textbook passage before writing them down.

Option 2: Have students work independently to fill in the missing letters in Activity 2. Then, ask them to write the answers to Activity 3 in their notebooks, circling the keywords in the questions first.

Why did you choose that specific word as your keyword to hunt in the passage?

Differentiation:

  • Support: Provide sentence starters on the board for the Activity 3 answers (e.g., "Sir Riaz said freedom means...", "Pinky also went to ask...").
  • Challenge: Ask early finishers to write one new comprehension question about the passage and trade it with a partner to solve using Keyword Hunting.

Teacher Support - Answer Key:

  • Activity 2: Freedom, Rhythmic, Nervousness, Cherish, Sacrifice.
  • Activity 3 (Answers will vary based on the preceding text, but generally):
  • 1. (Look for 'Sir Riaz' in text) Sir Riaz said freedom means [text detail].
  • 2. (Look for 'Pinky' and 'ask') Pinky went to ask [text detail].
  • 3. (Look for 'Pinky' and 'protect') It is important to protect freedom because [text detail].
  • 4. (Open-ended) Freedom means being able to make good choices and live peacefully.

6. Conclusion 🏁 (3 Minutes)

AFL Strategy: Traffic Lights. Provide students with red, yellow, and green cards. Ask them to hold up Green if they mastered Keyword Hunting, Yellow if they need a little more practice, and Red if they are confused.

Final Assessment Questions:

  1. Who is the first person mentioned in Activity 3 that talks about freedom?
  2. What does the text say we must do with our freedom, according to Pinky?
  3. Who did Pinky speak to besides Sir Riaz?
  4. Why do you think a yellow star is used next to the last question about what freedom means to you?
