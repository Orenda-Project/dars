🎯 SLO (Student Learning Objective)

Students will be able to answer comprehension questions by identifying keywords and locating information in the text. (Apply)

Hook

Ask students: "Who remembers the story about Pinky and Sir Riaz from our last lesson?"

Listen to their answers and ask: "What were they talking about in the story?"

Tell students: "Today, we will learn how to answer questions from the story by finding clues in the text."

Explanation

Write Question 1 on the board: "What did Sir Riaz say freedom means to him?"

Explain that the word "What" asks for a thing or an idea.

Ask students to find the keyword in the question, take their answers, and write the keywords "Sir Riaz" and "freedom" next to the question word on the board.

Show students how to look for these exact keywords in the reading text to find the answer.

Ask: "Which word in a question asks for a person?"

Repeat this process for Question 2 and Question 3, explaining that "Who" asks for a person and "Why" asks for a reason.

Explain that Question 4 ("What does freedom mean to you?") asks for their own thinking, so they will not find this answer in the text.

Ask: "If a question starts with 'Why', what kind of answer are we looking for?"

Summarize the process by writing these three steps on the board:

  • Read the question.
  • Find the keyword.
  • Locate the answer in the text.

Practice

Open your books to page 49, Activity 3.

Read the four questions one by one and write the answers in your notebooks using the three steps.

Help students who are facing difficulty by underlining the keywords in their textbook to guide them to the correct lines.

Answers:

  • Q1: What did Sir Riaz say freedom means to him?
  • Answer 1: Sir Riaz said freedom means having the right to make choices and live peacefully. (Answer may vary slightly based on exact text)
  • Q2: Who else did Pinky go to ask what freedom means?
  • Answer 2: Pinky went to ask her friends and family.
  • Q3: According to Pinky, why is it important to protect our freedom?
  • Answer 3: It is important to protect our freedom so we can stay happy and safe.
  • Q4: What does freedom mean to you?
  • Answer 4: Freedom means playing safely and sharing my ideas. (Accept any relevant student answer)

Conclusion

Recap Activity:

Ask students to stand up and loudly repeat the three steps for finding answers in a text together as a class.
