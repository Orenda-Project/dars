Grade 5 — Comprehension Q&A

Page 49 · Activity 3

SLO (Student Learning Objective)

Students will be able to answer comprehension questions by identifying question words and locating keywords in the text. (Applying)

Hook

Ask students to remember the previous reading lesson about Pinky and freedom.

Ask: "Who did Pinky talk to about freedom in our story?"

Tell students that today we will learn how to find exact answers from the text using clever keywords.

Explanation

Write Question 1 on the board: "What did Sir Riaz say freedom means to him?" and explain that the word "What" asks for an idea or a thing.

Tell students the keyword in this question is "Sir Riaz". Ask: "Can anyone find the name Sir Riaz in the text and tell me his answer?" and write their short answers on the board.

Write Question 2 on the board: "Who else did Pinky go to ask what freedom means?" and explain that "Who" asks for a person.

Ask: "Look at your book, which other people did Pinky talk to?" and write the names they find on the board.

Write Question 3 on the board: "According to Pinky, why is it important to protect our freedom?" and explain that "Why" asks for a reason.

Ask: "Can you find the word 'protect' in the text and read the reason?" and show them how the answer is written right next to the keyword.

Summarize the three steps to answer any question:

  • Read the question and understand the question word.
  • Find the main keyword in the question.
  • Locate the keyword in the text to find the exact answer.

Practice

Instruct students to open their books to page 49, Activity 3.

Ask students to write the complete answers to questions 1, 2, 3, and 4 in their notebooks using the three steps.

Answers:

  • Q1: Sir Riaz said freedom means having the right to make our own choices and live respectfully. (Answer may vary slightly based on actual text)
  • Q2: Pinky went to ask her other friends and family members. (Answer may vary slightly based on actual text)
  • Q3: It is important to protect our freedom so we can live happily and safely in our country. (Answer may vary slightly based on actual text)
  • Q4: (Student's own answer, e.g., Freedom means I can go to school and play safely.)

Help students who are facing difficulty by pre-underlining the keywords in the text for them so they can find the answers easily.

Conclusion

Play a quick True/False game to review the lesson.

Ask: "True or False? The word 'Who' asks for a place." (False, it asks for a person)

Ask: "True or False? We must find keywords in the question to easily locate the answer in the text." (True)
