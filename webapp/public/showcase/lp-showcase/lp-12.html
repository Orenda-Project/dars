Teacher Steps
Predict / Activate8–11 min
Write the word 'treasure' on the board. Ask Grade 4: 'What do you already know about this word? What does it mean?' Take 2-3 oral responses. Then say: 'Good — now try to write one sentence using the word treasure in your notebook before I explain.' (اردو: بچو، یہ لفظ 'treasure' دیکھو — کیا تم پہلے سے جانتے ہو؟ اب اس پر ایک جملہ نوٹ بک میں بناؤ۔)
Independent Attempt11–17 min
Grade 4 attempts the task in silence. Teacher circulates and reads notebooks WITHOUT correcting. Identify if students are just writing the word without a sentence frame, or struggling with spelling. Ask: 'What do most students get wrong here?' (اردو: خاموشی سے اپنا کام کرو — استاد دیکھیں گے، ابھی مدد نہیں ملے گی۔)
Consolidate17–25 min
Say 'I saw many of you just wrote the word alone, or wrote incomplete sentences — the correct form is to use a starter like "I have..." or "This is..."'. Write 'treasure' (crossed out) and 'This is a treasure.' (with a tick) on the board. Say: 'A sentence tells a full idea.' Students correct their own notebook. Do choral repetition of the correct sentence 2 times. (اردو: بچو، بہت سوں نے صرف لفظ لکھا — صحیح یہ ہے 'This is a treasure'۔ اپنی نوٹ بک میں درست کرو اور ایک بار بلند آواز سے پڑھو۔)
Quick Check25–27 min
Give ONE new word: 'mouse'. Say: 'Write a sentence with the word mouse.' Students write in notebooks. Ask 2 students to read their answer aloud. Confirm they used a full sentence. (اردو: یہ لفظ 'mouse' پر جملہ مکمل کرو — پھر بلند آواز سے پڑھو۔)
Transition (27–30 min): Clap twice loudly.
Grade 4, look at your board and start your independent work. Grade 5, put your pencils down and look at me. (اردو: گریڈ 4، اپنا بورڈ کا کام شروع کرو۔ گریڈ 5، پنسل چھوڑو اور میری طرف دیکھو۔)

Assign one strong reader in Grade 4 and one in Grade 5 as Group Leaders. During Independent (I) blocks, the Group Leader reads the board instructions aloud once to their group, ensures everyone opens the correct textbook page, and points to the words on the board if someone is stuck.
