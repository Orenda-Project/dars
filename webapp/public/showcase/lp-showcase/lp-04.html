Grade 2 — Grammar

Page 10 · Activity 3

Lesson Objective

Students will be able to use the articles 'a', 'an', and 'the' correctly in sentences. (Application)

Hook

  • Hold up a pencil in one hand and an apple (or an eraser) in the other hand.
  • Ask: "What is in my right hand and what is in my left hand?"
  • Say: "Today we will learn how to use small words like 'a', 'an', and 'the' before these naming words to make our sentences sound right."

Explanation

  • Write the letters a, e, i, o, u on the board and tell students we use 'an' before words starting with these sounds, like 'an apple' or 'an egg'.
  • Explain that we use 'a' before words starting with all other letters, like 'a book' or 'a pencil'.
  • Ask: "Do we say 'a cat' or 'an cat'?"
  • Tell students we use 'the' when we talk about a specific thing or a place we all know, like 'the park' or 'the sun'.
  • Ask: "Can someone give another example of a word that uses 'an'?"

Practice

  • Write the Activity 3 sentences on the board and read them aloud.
  • Ask students to copy the sentences in their notebooks and fill in the blanks with 'a', 'an', or 'the'.
  • Help students who are facing difficulty by underlining the first letter of the naming word in the sentence.
  • Check the answers together as a class.
Questions:
  1. Pinky has ___ apple.
  2. Jojo saw ___ owl.
  3. They went to ___ park together.
  4. Sir Riaz bought ___ umbrella for the rain.
  5. There's ___ egg in the basket.
  6. Jojo is reading ___ book about stars.
  7. Pinky wants to visit ___ zoo to see ___ elephant.
  8. Sir Riaz teaches in ___ school near ___ old library.
Answers:
  1. an
  2. an
  3. the
  4. an
  5. an
  6. a
  7. the, an
  8. a, an

Conclusion

True/False Activity:

  • Ask: "Tell me if this is true or false: We use 'an' before the word 'umbrella'."
  • Ask: "Tell me if this is true or false: We say 'an book' when we read."
