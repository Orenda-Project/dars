🎯 Student Learning Objective (SLO)

🎯 Students will be able to fill in the blanks using the correct articles 'a', 'an', and 'the' before naming words. (Application)

Hook

Hold a pencil and an eraser in your hands.

Ask: "What is the first sound you hear when I say the word 'eraser'?"

Tell students today we will learn to use small words like 'a', 'an', and 'the' before naming words.

Explanation

Write vowels (a, e, i, o, u) on the board and tell students we use 'an' before words starting with these sounds, like 'an eraser' or 'an eye'.

Show a pencil to the class and explain we use 'a' before words starting with other sounds, like 'a pencil' or 'a book'.

Explain that we use 'the' for specific places or things we all know about, like 'the sun' or 'the park'.

Ask: "Will we say 'a orange' or 'an orange'?"

Ask: "Why do we say 'the moon' and not 'a moon'?"

Practice

Write the 8 fill-in-the-blank sentences from Activity 3 on the board.

Ask students to copy the sentences in their notebooks and write 'a', 'an', or 'the' in the blanks.

Read each sentence aloud slowly and ask students to share their answers to check together.

Help students who are facing difficulty by underlining the first letter of the naming word and asking them to check if it is a vowel.

Questions and Answers:

  • Q1: Pinky has ____ apple.
  • Answer 1: an
  • Q2: Jojo saw ____ owl.
  • Answer 2: an
  • Q3: They went to ____ park together.
  • Answer 3: the
  • Q4: Sir Riaz bought ____ umbrella for the rain.
  • Answer 4: an
  • Q5: There's ____ egg in the basket.
  • Answer 5: an
  • Q6: Jojo is reading ____ book about stars.
  • Answer 6: a
  • Q7: Pinky wants to visit ____ zoo to see ____ elephant.
  • Answer 7: the, an
  • Q8: Sir Riaz teaches in ____ school near ____ old library.
  • Answer 8: a, an

Conclusion

Play a quick True or False game to review the lesson.

Ask: "True or False: We use 'a' before the word 'ice cream'."

Ask: "True or False: We use 'the' before the word 'sky'."
