Grade 2 — Reading

Page 111 · Journey through text

Student Learning Objective

Students will be able to read the text aloud and identify the main idea of the story. (Comprehension)

Hook

Ask students: "Have you ever lost your mother or father in a big market?"

Listen to two or three short answers from the students.

Tell students that today we will read a story about a girl named Pinky who gets lost in a grocery store.

Explanation

Write and explain the meanings of difficult words on the board: grocery (a shop for food), butterfly (an insect with colourful wings), fluttery (moving lightly and fast), address (the place where you live).

Read the whole story "Pinky's Store Adventure!" aloud with expression, pointing to the words in the book as you read.

Explain that the main idea of the text is about staying brave when you get lost and remembering your home address and phone number.

Ask: What was Pinky looking at when her mother walked away?

Ask: What did Pinky's father tell her to do if she gets lost?

Practice

Pick a paragraph randomly from the text.

Ask students to read the paragraph independently.

Listen carefully and correct their pronunciation if they make a mistake.

Note for differentiated learners: Help students who are facing difficulty by reading the sentence first and asking them to repeat it after you.

Conclusion

Play a quick True/False game to review the lesson.

  • Question 1: Pinky got lost in the park. True or False?
  • Answer 1: False
  • Question 2: Pinky's father told her to learn their phone number. True or False?
  • Answer 2: True
