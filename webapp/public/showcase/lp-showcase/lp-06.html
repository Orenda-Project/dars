Grade 5 — Reading

Page 32-33 · Journey through text

Student Learning Objective

Students will be able to read the story "Being Res-paw-nsible!" aloud with correct pronunciation and identify the main idea of the text (Understanding).

Hook

  • Ask students: "Do you have any pets at home, or what pet do you wish to have?"
  • Listen to a few answers from the students to check what they know.
  • Tell students that today we will read a story about a girl named Pinky and her pet cat.

Explanation

  • Write and explain difficult words on the board: responsibilities (duties), infection (illness), properly (correctly), and ignore (not pay attention).
  • Read the story "Being Res-paw-nsible!" aloud slowly using the "Model Reading" strategy while students follow the text with their fingers.
  • Explain the main idea of the story, which is that taking care of a pet is a big duty and we must not ignore their needs.
  • Ask: What did Pinky name her cat?
  • Ask: Why did Whiskers get sick?

Practice

  • Pick a random paragraph in the story and ask a student to read it independently.
  • Listen carefully to the reading and gently correct any pronunciation mistakes.
  • Note for differentiated learners: Help students who are facing difficulty by reading the hard words first and asking them to repeat after you.

Conclusion

  • Play a quick True or False game to review the reading lesson.
  • Q1: True or False? Pinky took great care of Whiskers until the end.
  • Answer 1: False
  • Q2: True or False? Whiskers got sick because he was not eating.
  • Answer 2: True
