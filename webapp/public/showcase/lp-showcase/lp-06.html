🎯 Student Learning Objective (SLO)

Students will be able to read the text "Being Res-paw-nsible!" with correct pronunciation and identify the main idea of the story. (Comprehension)

Hook

Ask students: "Have you ever wanted a pet, and what kind of pet was it?"

Listen to two or three students share their answers.

Tell the students we will read a story about a girl who got a pet cat and what happened next.

Explanation

Write these difficult words on the board and explain their meanings simply: responsibility (a duty to care), infection (a sickness), properly (in a correct way), and promised (gave your word).

Read the story aloud to the class using clear expression, pausing at commas and full stops to show how to read properly.

Ask: "Why did Pinky name her cat Whiskers?"

Tell the students that the main idea of the text is that having a pet is a big duty, and we must take care of them even when we are tired.

Ask: "What happened to Whiskers when Pinky stopped taking care of him?"

Practice

Pick a student randomly and ask them to read the first paragraph of the story independently.

Call another student randomly to read the next paragraph aloud to the class.

Continue picking students randomly until the whole story is read.

Note for differentiated learners: If a student is facing difficulty reading a long word, read the word first and ask them to repeat after you.

Conclusion

Ask students to listen and say "True" or "False" for these sentences:

  • Q1: Pinky took good care of Whiskers every day and never forgot to feed him.
  • Answer 1: False
  • Q2: Pinky learned that caring for someone means thinking about their needs.
  • Answer 2: True
