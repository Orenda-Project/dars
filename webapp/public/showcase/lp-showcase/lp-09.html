SLO (Student Learning Objective)

Students will be able to complete sentences using reciprocal pronouns correctly. (Application)

Hook

  • Call two students to the front of the class and ask them to shake hands.
  • Ask: "What are they doing with...?" (Wait for students to say "each other").
  • Tell students that today they will learn about special words like "each other" that show teamwork.

Explanation

  • Write the heading "Reciprocal Pronouns" on the board.
  • Explain that reciprocal pronouns show teamwork and how people do things together.
  • Write the words "each other" and "one another" on the board.
  • Give a real-life example by saying: "Ali and Asad share lunch with each other."
  • Ask: What do reciprocal pronouns show?
  • Ask: Can someone make a simple sentence using "each other"?

Practice

  • Write the incomplete sentences from Activity 3 on the board.
  • Instruct students to copy the sentences into their notebooks.
  • Tell students to complete the sentences using "each other" or "one another".

Questions:

  • Q1: The cats chased...
  • Q2: The teammates supported...
  • Q3: The dancers admired...

Answers:

  • Answer 1: each other.
  • Answer 2: one another.
  • Answer 3: one another.

Note for differentiated learners: Help students who are facing difficulty by translating the meaning of "each other" into their mother tongue and giving them an example with their own names.

Conclusion

  • Conduct a quick True/False quiz to review the lesson.
  • Ask: "Reciprocal pronouns show people doing things together. True or False?" (True)
  • Ask: "The word 'tree' is a reciprocal pronoun. True or False?" (False)
