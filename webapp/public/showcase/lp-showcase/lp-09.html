SLO (Student Learning Objective)

🎯 Students will be able to use reciprocal pronouns to complete sentences showing teamwork or shared actions. (Applying)

Hook

Call two students to the front of the class and ask them to shake hands.

Ask: "Who are they shaking hands with?"

Tell the students that today we will learn about special words that show teamwork and doing things together.

Explanation

Write the words "each other" and "one another" on the board and tell students these are called reciprocal pronouns.

Explain that we use these words when people do the same action together, like "Ali and Sara help each other."

Ask: Can someone give an example of a time you shared your lunch with a friend?

Give another example by saying, "All the students in the class respect one another."

Ask: What word did I use to show the whole class doing the same thing together?

Practice

Instruct students to open page 39 and look at Activity 3.

Ask students to complete the sentences in their notebooks using the reciprocal pronouns "each other" or "one another".

Check the answers together as a class by writing them on the board.

Questions and Answers:

  • Q1: The cats chased...
  • Answer 1: each other.
  • Q2: The teammates supported...
  • Answer 2: one another.
  • Q3: The dancers admired...
  • Answer 3: each other.

Help students who are facing difficulty by asking them to point out the people or animals in the sentence who are doing the action together.

Conclusion

Play a quick True/False game to review the lesson.

  • Q1: We use reciprocal pronouns to show people doing things alone.
  • Answer 1: False
  • Q2: "Each other" is an example of a reciprocal pronoun.
  • Answer 2: True
