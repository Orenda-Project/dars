Grade 5 — Creative writing

Page 15 · Activity 3

Student Learning Objective

Students will be able to write five sentences about a personal superpower using correct capitalization and three 3-syllable words. (Create)

Hook

Ask: "If you could do magic, what one special thing would you do?"

Listen to answers from two or three students.

Tell students that today we will write sentences about our own special powers, which we call superpowers!

Explanation

Draw a mind map on the board with "My Superpower" in the center and write simple ideas like "reading fast" or "helping others" in circles around it.

Explain that when we write our five sentences, every sentence must start with a big capital letter and end with a full stop.

Ask: Which letter will be capital in the word "superman" if it is the first word of a sentence?

Write an example sentence on the board using a 3-syllable word, like: "I have a-maz-ing powers."

Ask: Can someone tell me another word that has three claps or syllables?

Practice

Tell students to look at Activity 3 in their books and open their notebooks to draw a quick mind map about their own superpower.

Instruct them to write five sentences about their superpower using their mind map ideas.

Remind them to start sentences with capital letters and include three words that have 3 syllables (write these examples on the board: won-der-ful, en-er-gy, mag-i-cal).

Note for differentiated learners: Help students who are facing difficulty by giving them simple sentence starters like "My power is..." and providing a small list of 3-syllable words on the board to choose from.

Conclusion

Conduct a quick True/False activity to review the lesson.

  • Q1: True or False: We start a new sentence with a small letter.
  • Answer 1: False
  • Q2: True or False: The word "won-der-ful" has three syllables.
  • Answer 2: True
