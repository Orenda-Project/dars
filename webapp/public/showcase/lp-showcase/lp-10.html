🎯 Student Learning Objective

Students will be able to write five capitalized sentences about their imaginary superpower using at least three 3-syllable words. (Creating)

Hook

Ask: If you could have any magic power, what would you choose?

Listen to a few student answers to check their imagination.

Tell the class: Today, we will write our own short story about our superpower.

Explanation

Draw a simple mind map on the board with a circle in the center saying "My Superpower".

Write ideas around the circle, like "flying", and add 3-syllable words like "won-der-ful" or "pow-er-ful".

Show students how to write a complete sentence from the mind map starting with a capital letter, like: "I have a pow-er-ful magic."

Ask: What must we put at the beginning of every sentence?

Ask: Can someone tell me another word that has exactly three syllables?

Practice

Ask students to open page 15 and look at Activity 3.

Tell students to draw a quick mind map in their notebooks to plan their superpower ideas.

Instruct them to write five sentences about their power, using capital letters and three 3-syllable words.

Example 3-syllable words to write on board: pow-er-ful, mag-i-cal, won-der-ful, a-maz-ing.

Note for differentiated learners: For students facing difficulty, write sentence starters on the board like "My superpower is..." and help them count syllables on their fingers.

Conclusion

Play a quick True/False game to review the lesson rules.

  • Q1: We start a sentence with a small letter. True or False?
  • Answer 1: False
  • Q2: The word "won-der-ful" has three syllables. True or False?
  • Answer 2: True
