Journey through text — Multigrade Lesson (Grade 2, Grade 3)

🕐 Opening 0–8 min | 2 rotations × 22 min | Closing 52–60 min|📦 Textbooks for Grade 2 and Grade 3 · Small pebbles or seeds for keeping track of found words (Grade 2) · Chalkboard with pre-written sentence starters and crossword clues (Grade 3)

Learning Objectives by Grade

GradeLearning Objective
Grade 2Students will be able to read aloud simple sentences and visually scan to find specific words (ending in 's' or starting with 'T') using textbook page 111.
Grade 3Students will be able to read a short text and answer simple comprehension questions using sentence starters based on textbook pages 127-128.

Combined Opening — All Grades Together (8 min) 0–8 min

Hook0–2 min
Teacher walks to the center of the room, looks around with wide eyes, and pretends to be lost, calling out 'Mama? Abbu?' in a worried voice. Suddenly, smile and say, 'Have you ever felt like this in a big market?' End with: 'Today we will read about a girl who got lost, and an adventure in the mountains! Aaj yahi seekhein ge!' (اردو: بچو، دیکھو مجھے! ماما؟ ابو؟ کیا آپ کبھی بازار میں ایسے گم ہوئے ہو؟ آج ہم ایک لڑکی کی کہانی پڑھیں گے جو گم ہو گئی تھی، اور پہاڑوں کی سیر بھی کریں گے! آج یہی سیکھیں گے!)
Prior Knowledge Trigger2–4 min
Ask the whole class one quick question about going to new places. 'When you go to a big shop or a park, what do you like to see?' Point to Grade 2 to shout out one word, then Grade 3 to shout out a short phrase. Take 3-4 answers quickly and move on. (اردو: بچو، ہاتھ اٹھاؤ! جب آپ کسی بڑی دکان یا پارک میں جاتے ہو تو کیا دیکھنا پسند کرتے ہو؟ کوئی بھی جواب دو!)
SLO Share4–6 min
Point to the Grade 2 board and say, 'Grade 2, you will find special words in Pinky's story.' Point to the Grade 3 board and say, 'Grade 3, you will answer questions about an adventure in Hunza.' (اردو: گریڈ 2، آپ پنکی کی کہانی میں سے خاص الفاظ ڈھونڈو گے۔ گریڈ 3، آپ ہنزہ کی سیر کے سوالوں کے جواب دو گے۔)
Board Orientation6–7 min
Point to each grade's section on the board. Ask the group leaders to stand up, check their groups, and tell everyone to open their English notebooks and textbooks. (اردو: بچو، بورڈ کی طرف دیکھو۔ گروپ لیڈرز کھڑے ہو جائیں اور سب کی کاپیاں اور کتابیں کھلوائیں۔)
Warm-up (All Grades Simultaneously)7–8 min
Tell all students to look at the board, find the word 'Pinky', and copy it three times in their notebooks beautifully. Observe them as they write. (اردو: سب بچے بورڈ سے 'Pinky' کا لفظ دیکھ کر اپنی کاپی میں تین بار لکھو۔ جلدی کرو!)

Board Setup (Before Class)

Teacher Steps
Engage8–11 min
Ask Grade 2 students to look at the picture on page 111. 'What do you see in the picture? Is Pinky happy or scared?' Let them shout out answers. Tell Grade 3 to start their board work silently. (اردو: گریڈ 2، صفحہ 111 پر تصویر دیکھو۔ تصویر میں کیا ہے؟ کیا پنکی خوش ہے یا ڈری ہوئی؟ گریڈ 3، اپنا کام خاموشی سے شروع کرو۔)
Input / Model11–17 min
Read the first paragraph of 'Pinky's Store Adventure' aloud with a lot of expression. Tell students to put their fingers on the words. Read it again and have them repeat chorally. Then say, 'Let's find a word ending with s. Look at the word kisses.' Write 'kisses' on the board. (اردو: بچو، میری بات غور سے سنو اور کتاب پر انگلی رکھو۔ اب میرے ساتھ پڑھو۔ آؤ ایک ایسا لفظ ڈھونڈیں جس کے آخر میں 's' آتا ہو۔ دیکھو، 'kisses'۔ اسے کاپی میں لکھو۔)
Guided Practice17–25 min
Tell students to use their pebbles to mark words in the second paragraph that start with 'T'. Write the frame 'T____' on the board. Ask them to find words like 'tall' or 'tummy' and copy them into the blanks on the board and in their notebooks. Walk around and help them scan. (اردو: اب اپنی کتاب میں وہ الفاظ ڈھونڈو جو 'T' سے شروع ہوتے ہیں، جیسے 'tall'۔ ان پر کنکر رکھو اور پھر بورڈ سے دیکھ کر کاپی میں خالی جگہ پر لکھو۔)
Quick Check25–27 min
Ask two students to stand up and read one word they found that ends with 's' and one that starts with 'T'. Give a thumbs up if correct. (اردو: آپ دونوں کھڑے ہو جاؤ۔ ایک لفظ بتاؤ جس کے آخر میں 's' ہو، اور ایک جو 'T' سے شروع ہو۔ بہت خوب!)
Transition (27–30 min): Clap three times loudly.
Grade 2, keep writing your words from the board. Grade 3, pencils down and look at me! (اردو: گریڈ 2، بورڈ سے دیکھ کر اپنے الفاظ لکھتے رہو۔ گریڈ 3، پنسل چھوڑ دو اور میری طرف دیکھو!)
✅ Check that Grade 2 students are looking at their books/board and writing before turning fully to Grade 3.

Peer Tutoring Setup

Assign the most confident reader in Grade 3 as the group leader. Assign a focused student in Grade 2 as their leader. During I/P blocks, the leader reads the board instructions aloud once, ensures everyone is on the correct textbook page, and points to the words on the board if a peer is stuck.
