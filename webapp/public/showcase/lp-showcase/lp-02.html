Lesson plan

Grade 2 — Comprehension w/ meanings

Page 15 · New words to know

Student Learning Objective

Students will be able to identify and use five new vocabulary words (cheerful, magic, share, tummies, flavours) correctly. (Applying)

Hook

Show the picture given on page 15 of the textbook to the class.

Ask: "What do you see happening in this picture?" to check what they already know.

Tell the students that today we will learn new words to talk about this picture and our food.

Explanation

Read the word "cheerful" aloud and smile big to show the meaning to the students.

Ask: "Who looks cheerful in our class today?"

Explain "share" by giving your pencil to a student, and explain "tummies" by rubbing your stomach to show bellies.

Explain "magic" as a special trick, and "flavours" as different tastes like sweet mango or spicy biryani.

Ask: "What is your favourite ice cream flavour?"

Practice

Write three fill-in-the-blank sentences on the board.

Ask students to read the sentences and write the correct word (cheerful, share, tummies) in their notebooks.

  • Q1: I like to _____ my lunch with my friend.
  • Q2: Ali is happy and _____.
  • Q3: When we eat food, it goes in our _____.

Answers:

  • Answer 1: share
  • Answer 2: cheerful
  • Answer 3: tummies

Help students who are facing difficulty by reading the sentences slowly and translating them into Urdu for better understanding.

Conclusion

Play a quick True or False game to review the words.

  • Q1: True or False: "Share" means keeping all the food for yourself.
  • Answer 1: False
  • Q2: True or False: "Cheerful" means you are happy and smiling.
  • Answer 2: True
