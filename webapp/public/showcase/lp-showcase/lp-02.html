Grade 2 — Comprehension w/ meanings

Page 15 · New words to know

Objectives (2 Minutes)

  • The Refined SLO: Students will be able to define words and use them to construct original sentences. [Understand & Apply].

Resources & Teacher Support 💡 (0 Minutes)

  • Flashcards of the focus words, whiteboard, and colorful markers.
  • Focus words: cheerful, magic, share, tummies, flavours.
  • Visual Anchor: A Word Web drawn on the board with "Lunchtime" in the center bubble, branching out to the focus words with small simple drawings (like a smiley face for cheerful).
  • Teacher Note: Connect physical actions to the vocabulary words to build muscle memory. Ask the class: Who has ever shared their favorite lunch with a friend?

Introduction 🚀 (5 Minutes)

  • Show the class a small, brightly colored lunchbox with a big smiley face drawn on it. Give it a little shake so it rattles slightly.
  • Good morning, my wonderful students! Look at this special lunchbox. Ali, what do you think is hiding inside? Today, we are going to unlock some amazing words about a very magical lunchtime!

Explanation & Teaching 🧠 (15 Minutes)

  • Teach the words using physical movements. For 'cheerful', show a huge smile and point to it. For 'magic', wiggle fingers like casting a spell. For 'share', pretend to hand a piece of food to a neighbor. For 'tummies', rub the belly in a circle. For 'flavours', pretend to taste something delicious and tap your lips.
  • Draw a bubble map on the board with the word 'cheerful' in the middle. Ask the students to help fill the connecting bubbles with related words like 'happy', 'smiling', and 'glad'.
  • Write the sentence formula clearly on the board: (Who/What + Action + Description).
  • I want to make a sentence using the word 'share'. Let me think out loud. Who will be my subject? Zainab. What is her action? Shares her lunch. What is the description? With her friends. So my sentence is: Zainab shares her lunch with her friends!
  • CFU:
    • What does the word 'cheerful' mean? [Answer: Feeling happy and showing it by smiling.]
    • Show me the gesture for 'tummies'. [Answer: Students rub their bellies.]
    • In the sentence 'Ahmed eats sweet flavours', what is the action? [Answer: eats.]

Practice ✍️ (15 Minutes)

Your minds are overflowing with stories. Let's capture them on paper!

The teacher may choose one or more of the following options based on the class's needs and available time:

  • Option 1: (Pair Brainstorming & Oral Sentence Sharing). Students sit with a partner, pick two words from the board, and take turns saying sentences out loud using the formula.
  • Option 2: (Group Sentence Race on the board). Call small groups to the board to quickly write one sentence for a chosen focus word, checking for Who + Action + Description.
  • Option 3: (Individual Focused Writing in notebooks). Students independently write three sentences using three different focus words in their notebooks.
  • Differentiation:
    • Support: Provide sentence starters on the board (e.g., "The magic ___", "Fatima feels ___").
    • Challenge: Ask students to use 2 focus words in one single sentence (e.g., "Ali is cheerful when he shares his food.").

Conclusion 🏁 (3 Minutes)

  • Use a 1-2-3 Finger check to assess understanding. Hold up 1 finger if you need help, 2 fingers if you are almost there, and 3 fingers if you completely understand our new words!
  • Ask the following questions to verify skills:
    • What word means another word for our bellies?
    • What does 'magic' mean?
    • Use 'cheerful' in a short sentence.
    • What are different tastes of food called?
  • Pick 2-3 students (like Ahmed and Fatima) to read their best sentence out loud. Ask the class to listen and identify the "Who" and the "Action" in those sentences.
  • You all did a magical job today! Keep sharing those cheerful smiles with everyone you meet!
