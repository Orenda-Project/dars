Grade 2 — Comprehension w/ meanings

Page 15 · New words to know

Objectives (2 Minutes)

  • Students will be able to define words and use them to construct original sentences. [Understand & Apply].

Resources & Teacher Support 💡

  • Flashcards with the 5 focus words.
  • A large illustration of children sharing a meal.
  • Focus words: cheerful, magic, share, tummies, flavours.
  • Visual Anchor: A "Word Web" drawn on the board with the word "cheerful" in the center, surrounded by bubbles containing "smiling," "happy," "playing," and "laughing."
  • Teacher Note: Use full-body physical gestures to help students remember abstract meanings, like rubbing your stomach for "tummies." Have you ever felt so happy that your tummy felt full before you even ate?

Introduction 🚀 (5 Minutes)

  • Good morning, my wonderful class! Today, we are going to open a magical lunchbox filled with new words!
  • Zainab, if you could have any magical food in your lunchbox, what would it be?
  • Listen to this quick story: Ali opened his bag and found a glowing apple. When he took a bite, he started laughing!
  • Let's look at the picture on page 15. What do you think is happening? Who do you see? Are there any foods you can spot?

Explanation & Teaching 🧠 (15 Minutes)

  • Let's learn our new lunchbox words using our bodies! Stand up!
  • For "cheerful," smile widely and point to your cheeks. For "magic," wiggle your fingers like casting a spell. For "share," hold out your hands as if giving a gift. For "tummies," rub your stomach. For "flavours," lick your lips and say "mmm."
  • Now, let's draw a map for our brain on the board.
  • Draw a bubble on the board with the word "share." Draw lines to new bubbles and write "friends," "give," and "half."
  • When we know what a word means, we can build a strong sentence. We use a secret recipe: (Who/What + Action + Description).
  • Write (Who/What + Action + Description) clearly on the board.
  • Watch me use our recipe for the word 'cheerful'. Hmm, who is cheerful? Ahmed! What is the action? Ahmed plays. Description? With a big smile. So my sentence is: Ahmed plays cheerfully with his friends.
  • CFU:
  • Q1: What physical action did we do for the word "tummies"?
  • [Answer Key: Rubbing our stomachs.]
  • Q2: If I give half of my sandwich to Fatima, which focus word am I doing?
  • [Answer Key: Share.]
  • Q3: In the sentence "Fatima eats the magic apple," who is the "Who"?
  • [Answer Key: Fatima.]

Practice ✍️ (15 Minutes)

  • Your minds are overflowing with stories. Let's capture them on paper!
  • The teacher may choose one or more of the following options based on the class's needs and available time:
  • Option 1: Pair Brainstorming & Oral Sentence Sharing. Students sit with their shoulder partner. One student picks a word card, and the other uses the Who + Action + Description formula to say a sentence out loud.
  • Option 2: Group Sentence Race on the board. Divide the class into teams. Call out a word like "flavours," and one student from each team races to the board to write a complete sentence using the formula.
  • Option 3: Individual Focused Writing in notebooks. Students write five original sentences, one for each focus word, making sure to circle the "Who" and underline the "Action."
  • Differentiation: Provide Support (Sentence starters on the board: "My tummy feels...", "Ali likes to share his...") and a Challenge (Write one long sentence that uses both "cheerful" and "magic").

Conclusion 🏁 (3 Minutes)

  • Show me your fingers! Hold up 1 finger if you need more help, 2 fingers if you understand a little, and 3 fingers if you are a word master!
  • Q1: What does "flavours" mean?
  • Q2: Can someone show me the action for "cheerful"?
  • Q3: What word means to give a part of something you have?
  • Q4: What is another word for our bellies?
  • Ali, please read your best sentence to the class. Class, listen closely and tell me the 'Who' and the 'Action'!
  • Zainab, share your sentence next!
  • You all did a fantastic job today. Keep using your magic words at lunchtime!
