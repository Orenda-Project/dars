🎯 SLO (Student Learning Objective)

Students will be able to tell the meanings of new vocabulary words (cheerful, magic, share, tummies, flavours) using simple examples. (Understanding)

Hook

  • Show the students the picture on page 15 of the textbook.
  • Ask: "What do you think is happening in this picture and what foods can you see?"
  • Tell the students that today we will learn new words about this picture to help us understand a magical story.

Explanation

  • Read each new word (cheerful, magic, share, tummies, flavours) loudly from the board and ask students to repeat after you.
  • Explain "cheerful" by making a big smile, and explain "share" by giving your pen to a student.
  • Ask: What do you look like when you feel cheerful?
  • Explain "tummies" by touching your stomach, "flavours" by talking about sweet mangoes or spicy biryani, and "magic" by hiding a coin in your hand.
  • Ask: Can someone name two different flavours of juice?

Practice

  • Write three incomplete sentences on the board:
    1. I am happy and _____.
    2. I like sweet _____.
    3. My _____ is full after eating food.
  • Ask students to pick the correct word from the board (cheerful, flavours, tummies) to complete the sentences.
  • Answers:
    1. cheerful
    2. flavours
    3. tummies
  • Help students who are facing difficulty by reading the sentences aloud and pointing to the word meanings on the board.

Conclusion

  • Play a quick True/False game to check what students remember.
  • Read these two sentences and ask students to say True or False aloud:
  • Questions:
    • Q1: "Share" means keeping all the food for yourself.
    • Q2: "Cheerful" means feeling happy and showing it by smiling.
  • Answers:
    • Answer 1: False
    • Answer 2: True
