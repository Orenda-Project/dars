🎯 Student Learning Objective (SLO)

Students will be able to guess the meanings of new vocabulary words (freedom, rhythmic, nervousness, cherish, sacrifice) by reading context clues in sentences. (Understanding)

Hook

Show a closed box or bag to the class and say there is a hidden object inside.

Give a simple hint like "It is sweet and we eat it after dinner," and ask them to guess what is inside.

Ask: "Have you ever used hints to find a hidden thing before?"

Tell students that today we will use hints in sentences, called clues, to find the hidden meanings of new words.

Explanation

Write the word "freedom" on the board and tell students it means being able to do what you want, like playing outside when your homework is done.

Read Clue 1 from the book aloud and show how the words "do whatever you wanted to do" act as a hint to understand the word "freedom".

Ask: "If you have complete freedom on Sunday, what will you do?"

Explain the other words using daily life examples: "rhythmic" is like the regular tick-tock of a clock, "nervousness" is feeling scared before a math test, "cherish" is loving a special toy, and "sacrifice" is giving your turn on the slide to a friend.

Ask: "Can someone share a time they felt nervousness?"

Practice

Open the textbook to pages 44 and 45 and look at the "Guess the Meaning" activity.

Read Clues 2, 3, 4, and 5 one by one and ask students to write what they think the word means on the blank lines based on the sentence hint.

Check the answers together as a class by writing them on the board.

  • Answer Clue 1 (Freedom): Doing whatever you want to do.
  • Answer Clue 2 (Rhythmic): Beating in a regular pattern or sounding like a drum.
  • Answer Clue 3 (Nervousness): Feeling worried or not calm.
  • Answer Clue 4 (Cherish): To keep close to your heart or love something.
  • Answer Clue 5 (Sacrifice): To give up something you like for someone else.

Help students who are facing difficulty by translating the sentence clues into their mother tongue (Urdu) to make the hint easier to understand.

Conclusion

Play a quick True/False game to review the lesson.

  • Say: "True or False: Cherish means to throw a gift away." (False)
  • Say: "True or False: Sacrifice means giving your favorite candy to your brother." (True)
