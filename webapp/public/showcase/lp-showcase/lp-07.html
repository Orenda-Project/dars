Grade 5 — Comprehension w/ meanings

Student Learning Objective (SLO)

Students will be able to guess the meanings of new vocabulary words using context clues from sentences. (Understand)

Hook

  • Show a closed pencil box to the class and ask: "Can you guess what is inside this box without opening it?"
  • Listen to their answers and explain that they are using clues to guess.
  • Tell the students that today we will use clues in sentences to guess the meanings of new words.

Explanation

  • Write the new words on the board: freedom, rhythmic, nervousness, cherish, sacrifice.
  • Explain each word using a simple real-life example (e.g., 'freedom' is having free time to play, 'nervousness' is how you feel before an exam, 'sacrifice' is giving your favorite pencil to a friend).
  • Ask: Who can tell me about a time they felt nervousness?
  • Read Clue 1 from the book aloud and show how the words "do whatever you wanted" help us guess the meaning of 'freedom'.
  • Ask: What other words in the sentence give us a hint about the meaning?

Practice

  • Ask students to open their books to pages 44 and 45.
  • Tell students to read clues 2, 3, 4, and 5 and write down what they think each word means in the blanks.
  • Review the answers together as a class. Answers:
    • Clue 1 (Freedom): Being able to do what you want.
    • Clue 2 (Rhythmic): A steady, repeating beat like a drum.
    • Clue 3 (Nervousness): Feeling worried or not calm.
    • Clue 4 (Cherish): To love something and keep it close to your heart.
    • Clue 5 (Sacrifice): Giving up something you like for someone else.
  • Help students who are facing difficulty by reading the sentences aloud slowly and pointing out familiar words.

Conclusion

True/False Activity

  • Say: "Listen carefully and tell me if this is True or False."
  • Q1: If you cherish a gift, you throw it in the dustbin.
  • Answer 1: False
  • Q2: A rhythmic sound beats in a regular way, like a drum.
  • Answer 2: True
