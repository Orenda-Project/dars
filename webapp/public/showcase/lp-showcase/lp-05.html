Grade 2 — Creative writing

Page 133

Learning Outcome (2 Minutes)

Students will be able to write a 3-sentence paragraph about Pinky's Deosai Plains adventure using descriptive words from a word bank. [Create].

Resources & Teacher Note 💡

  • Textbook page 133 (Pinky's Deosai Plains Adventure).
  • A large blank postcard drawn on chart paper.
  • Visual Sketches: 1. A large rectangle on the board looking like a postcard with a stamp in the corner. 2. A simple drawing of a big hill with a sun peeking out and a few flowers at the bottom.

Teacher Note: Encourage students to visualize themselves in the picture to make their writing come alive. Before starting, ask the class: Have you ever visited a beautiful green field or seen a big hill? What did it look like?

Opening 🚀 (5 Minutes)

Good morning, my wonderful explorers! Are you ready for an exciting trip today?

Hold up a postcard (or a piece of paper folded like one). Look at what arrived in our class mailbox! It is a postcard from a girl named Pinky. She is visiting a magical place in Pakistan called the Deosai Plains. Today, we are going to help Pinky write her postcard to tell everyone about the beautiful things she sees!

Explanation 🧠 (10 Minutes)

Draw a large circle on the board and write "Deosai Plains" in the middle. Draw lines coming out of the circle to create branches. At the end of each branch, write the "Power Words" from the book: big, grassy, pretty, flowers, calm, hills, open sky.

Let's look closely at Pinky's picture on page 133. I want to write a sentence about where she is. Let me think... I see green grass. I will look at my Power Words. Ah, 'grassy'! I will write: 'Pinky is in a grassy field.'

Now, I want to describe the hills. I see the words 'calm' and 'hills'. Let me build a sentence in my mind. 'The hills are calm.' Wait, I can make it even better by adding 'big'. 'I see big, calm hills.' That sounds like a perfect postcard sentence!

CFU:

  • What word can we use to describe the flowers? [Answer: pretty]
  • What kind of sky is in the picture? [Answer: open sky]
  • If Ali wants to write about the ground, which word should he use? [Answer: grassy]

Practice ✍️ (20 Minutes)

Your minds are overflowing with stories. Let's capture them on paper!

The teacher may choose one or more of the following options based on the class's needs and available time:

Option 1: (Collaborative Pair Brainstorming) Place students in pairs (e.g., Ali and Sara). Have them look at the picture together, point to the items (flowers, hills, sky), and verbally share sentences using the word bank before writing them down.

Option 2: (Individual Focused Writing) Students work independently in their notebooks to write 3 sentences, using the mind map on the board as a guide to check off words as they use them.

Option 3: (Interactive Postcard Creation) Give each table group a large paper postcard. Ahmed and Zainab can take turns writing one descriptive sentence each until the postcard has a complete 3-sentence paragraph.

Differentiation:

  • Support: Provide sentence starters on the board: "I am in a ___ place." "I see ___ hills." "There are ___ ___."
  • Challenge: Ask students to add one new describing word of their own that is not in the word bank (e.g., yellow sun, flying butterfly).

Swap your notebooks with your partner and put a tiny star next to the describing words they used!

Sample Paragraph: I am having fun in the grassy Deosai Plains. I can see big, calm hills and an open sky. There are pretty flowers growing everywhere!

Conclusion 🏁 (5 Minutes)

Everyone, if you used the word 'grassy' in your writing, wave your hands like grass in the wind! If you used the words 'open sky', point up to the ceiling!

Final Assessment Questions:

  1. Who is the girl in the picture?
  2. What is she holding in her hands?
  3. Name two things you can see in the Deosai Plains picture.
  4. Which describing word did we use for the hills?

Call on specific students to verify the skill: Ahmed, please stand up and read your favorite sentence about the flowers. Zainab, can you read your sentence about the sky?
