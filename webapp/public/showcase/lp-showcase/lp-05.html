🎯 SLO (Student Learning Objective)

Students will be able to write 2-3 simple sentences to describe a picture using given vocabulary words. (Applying)

Hook

Draw a simple picture of a hill, a sun, and a flower on the board.

Ask: "What things do you see in this drawing?"

Tell the students that today we will look at a picture of a beautiful place called Deosai Plains and write short sentences about it.

Explanation

Draw a big circle on the board and write "Deosai Plains" in the middle to make a mind map.

Read the words from the book (big, grassy, pretty flowers, calm hills, open sky) and write them in smaller circles around the main word.

Point to the words "pretty flowers" and write a simple sentence on the board like, "I see pretty flowers."

Ask: "Who can make a short sentence using the words 'open sky'?"

Ask: "Which word from our mind map can we use to talk about the hills?"

Practice

Open the book to page 133 and look at the picture of Pinky in the Deosai Plains.

Write 2 to 3 sentences about the picture in your notebooks using the words from the mind map.

Answers:

  • Answer 1: I see pretty flowers in the grassy field.
  • Answer 2: The calm hills are very big.
  • Answer 3: There is a beautiful open sky.

Help students who are facing difficulty by giving them simple sentence starters on the board, like "I see..." or "The hills are...".

Conclusion

Play a quick True or False game to review the lesson.

Questions:

  • Q1: We used the word 'grassy' to describe the picture today. (True)
  • Q2: We wrote sentences about a busy city with cars. (False)
