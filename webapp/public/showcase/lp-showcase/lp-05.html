Grade 2 — Creative writing

Page 133

Student Learning Objective (SLO)

Students will be able to write 2-3 simple sentences to describe a picture using given vocabulary words. (Application)

Hook

  • Show a simple picture from any book or draw a quick flower on the board.
  • Ask: "What do you see in this picture?"
  • Tell students: "Today, we will look at a beautiful picture of Deosai Plains and write sentences about it."

Explanation

  • Draw a big circle on the board and write "Deosai Plains" in the middle to make a mind map.
  • Read the words from the book (big, grassy, pretty, flowers, calm, hills, open, sky) and write them in smaller circles around the main circle.
  • Point to the picture on page 133 and show how to join a word from the mind map with what you see.
  • Write one example sentence on the board like "I see pretty flowers." to show how to start.

Checks for Understanding:

  • Ask: Who can make a short sentence with the word 'hills'?
  • Ask: What do we put at the end of a sentence?

Practice

  • Ask students to open page 133 and look at the picture of Pinky in the Deosai Plains.
  • Tell students to write 2-3 sentences about the picture using the words from the box.
  • Walk around the class to check their writing and guide them.

Expected Answers:

  • I see pretty flowers in the big grassy field.
  • There are calm hills under the open sky.

Note: Help students who are facing difficulty by asking them to verbally say what they see first, and then write "I see..." on their copies to help them start.

Conclusion

True/False Activity:

  • Q1: We use a full stop at the end of a sentence.
  • Answer 1: True
  • Q2: We write sentences without looking at the picture.
  • Answer 2: False
