Learning Outcome (2 Minutes)

Students will be able to write a 3-sentence paragraph about the Deosai Plains. [Create].

Resources & Teacher Note 💡

  • Textbook page 133
  • Chart paper and colorful markers
  • Visual Sketches: 1. A big green hill with colorful flowers. 2. A girl holding a camera under an open sky.

Teacher Note: Encourage students to vividly imagine the setting by connecting the vocabulary words to the visual illustration. Prior Knowledge Question: Have you ever visited a beautiful park or a hill station with lots of green grass?

Opening 🚀 (5 Minutes)

Good morning, my little explorers! Today, we are going on a magical trip without leaving our classroom!

Make a pretend camera with your hands and look through the lens! We are traveling to the beautiful Deosai Plains. What beautiful things do you see in your cameras? Do you see a butterfly? Do you see the sun? Let the students share their imaginary sights for a minute to build excitement.

Explanation 🧠 (10 Minutes)

Draw a large circle on the board and write "Deosai Plains" inside it. Draw branches coming out of the circle to create a mind map. Write the "Power Words" on the branches: big, grassy, pretty, flowers, calm, hills, open, sky.

Let me think about how to write my postcard. I want to tell my friend what I am looking at. I will write: I am standing in the big grassy plains. Now, what else do I see around me? Oh, the pretty flowers! I will add: There are pretty flowers on the calm hills. Let's finish by looking up. The open sky is so blue and beautiful! I will write: I love looking at the open sky.

CFU:

  • Q1: What kind of sky do we see?
  • Answer 1: Open sky.
  • Q2: Where are the pretty flowers growing?
  • Answer 2: On the calm hills / in the grassy plains.
  • Q3: What is Pinky holding in the picture?
  • Answer 3: A camera.

Practice ✍️ (20 Minutes)

Your minds are overflowing with stories. Let's capture them on paper!

The teacher may choose one or more of the following options based on the class's needs and available time:

Option 1: (Collaborative Pair/Group Brainstorming and Writing). Pair students up (e.g., Ali and Sara). Talk to your partner about what you want to write. Choose three power words together and write your sentences in your notebooks.

Option 2: (Individual Focused Writing with board support). Students like Ahmed and Zainab write independently. Look at the mind map on the board. Pick your favorite words and write three beautiful sentences on your own.

Option 3: (Interactive Board Writing). Who wants to come up and help me write one sentence on the board using the word 'butterfly'?

Differentiation:

  • Support: Provide sentence starters on the board: "I see big...", "The hills are...", "There is an open..."
  • Challenge: Ask early finishers to add one extra describing word that is not on the list (e.g., yellow sun, tiny butterfly).

Swap your notebooks with your partner and check if they used at least two words from our power word list!

Reference: "I am visiting the big grassy Deosai Plains. I see pretty flowers on the calm hills. The open sky is bright and sunny."

Conclusion 🏁 (5 Minutes)

Show me a thumbs up if you used the word 'grassy', and point up to the ceiling if you used the words 'open sky'!

  • Q1: What is the name of the place in our adventure?
  • Answer 1: Deosai Plains.
  • Q2: Which word describes the hills in our lesson?
  • Answer 2: Calm.
  • Q3: What are the flowers like?
  • Answer 3: Pretty.
  • Q4: What did you write about the sky?
  • Answer 4: It is open.

Select 2-3 students to read their favorite sentence out loud to prove they met the learning outcome.

You are all amazing writers and wonderful explorers! Keep using these beautiful power words in your stories!
