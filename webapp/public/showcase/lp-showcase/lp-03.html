Lesson Plan: Comprehension Questions

Objective

Students will be able to answer comprehension questions by finding keywords in the text. (Applying)

Hook

Ask: "Do you remember our story about the big cat in the cold mountains?"

Ask: "What was the color of that animal?"

Tell the students that today we will use tricks to find answers to Pinky's questions about the snow leopard.

Explanation

Explain the word "Describe" in Question 1 means to tell how something looks.

Help students find the keyword "appearance" or "look" in the question.

Ask students the question: "How does the snow leopard look?", take their answers, and write the key words from their answers next to the question word on the board.

Show how to find the answer in the text using the keyword "spots" or "big cat".

Explain the word "Where" in Question 2 asks for a place.

Help students find the keyword "live" in the question.

Ask students the question: "Where do snow leopards live?", take their answers, and write the key words next to the question word on the board.

Show how to find the answer in the text using the keyword "live" to find "cold mountains".

Explain the word "Which" in Question 3 asks for a specific name.

Help students find the keyword "food" or "animals" in the question.

Ask students the question: "What animals does it eat?", take their answers, and write the key words on the board.

Show how to find the answer in the text using the keyword "food".

Explain the word "What" in Question 4 asks about a thing or idea.

Help students find the keyword "remember" or "homes" in the question.

Ask students the question: "What must we remember about their homes?", take their answers, and write the key words on the board.

Show how to find the answer in the text using the keyword "homes".

Summarize the three steps to find answers:

  • Read the question.
  • Find the keyword.
  • Locate the answer in the text.

Practice

Ask students to open their books to Activity 2 and work in pairs.

Instruct students to find the keywords in the text and write the answers in their notebooks.

Share the correct answers on the board for students to check their work:

  • Answer 1: Snow leopards are big cats with spots.
  • Answer 2: They live up on tall, cold mountains.
  • Answer 3: They eat wild animals found in mountains.
  • Answer 4: We should remember to protect their homes.

Help students who are facing difficulty by pointing directly to the line in the book where the keyword is hiding.

Conclusion

Play a quick True/False game to check understanding.

Ask students to stand up for True and sit down for False.

  • Q1: The word "Where" asks us to look for a place. (True)
  • Q2: We do not need to look for keywords in the question. (False)
