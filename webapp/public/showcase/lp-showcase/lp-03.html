🎯 Student Learning Objective

Students will be able to find answers to questions by looking for keywords in the story about snow leopards. (Applying)

Hook

Ask students, "Who remembers what animal Pinky talked about in our last story?"

Ask students, "Was the snow leopard living in a hot jungle or cold mountains?"

Tell students that today we will use clues to find answers to Pinky's questions about the snow leopard.

Explanation

Write the question "Where do snow leopards love to live?" on the board.

Explain that the question word "Where" means we are looking for a place.

Help students find the keyword "live" in the question to locate the answer in the text.

Ask students the same question from the book, take their answers, and write the key words from their answers next to the question word on the board.

Show how to find the answer in the text using the keyword "live".

Repeat these steps for the question "Describe the appearance of a snow leopard" by explaining that "Describe" means to tell how it looks.

Ask: What keyword will we look for if the question asks about yummy food?

Summarize the three steps to find answers:

  • Read the question.
  • Find the keyword.
  • Locate the answer in the text.

Practice

Tell students to look at Activity 2 on page 128.

Ask students to find the keywords and write answers for question 3 and question 4 in their notebooks.

Check their work using these correct answers:

  • Q3: Which animals are on the snow leopard's yummy food list?
  • Answer 3: Markhor, blue sheep, and other small animals (based on previous story text).
  • Q4: What should we remember about snow leopards and their homes?
  • Answer 4: We should protect them and keep their tall, cold mountain homes clean and safe.

Help students who are facing difficulty by pointing to the exact paragraph where the keyword is hiding.

Conclusion

Play a quick True/False game to check understanding.

  • Q1: The word "Where" asks about a time.
  • Answer 1: False
  • Q2: We should find keywords in the question to help us get the answer.
  • Answer 2: True
