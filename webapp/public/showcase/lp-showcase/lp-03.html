Page 127-128 · Activity 2

Objectives (2 Minutes)

Students will be able to locate specific information using the Keyword Hunting strategy to answer comprehension questions. [Apply]

Resources & Teacher Support 💡

  • Textbook pages 127-128
  • Whiteboard and colorful whiteboard markers
  • Visual Anchor: Draw a large magnifying glass pointing to a bolded word on the board to represent "Keyword Hunting".

Teacher Note: Keyword hunting helps students focus on the most important words in a question, making it easier to scan the text for answers. When you lose a toy at home, do you look everywhere or do you look where you last played with it?

Introduction 🚀 (5 Minutes)

Good morning, my clever detectives! Are you ready to find some hidden clues today?

When you want to find your favorite cartoon on TV fast, what word or picture do you look for?

Word of the Day: Keyword – an important word that helps us unlock and find an answer quickly.

Let us look at Question 2 on page 127. The question asks, 'Where is the setting in Story 2?' The keyword here is 'setting', which means 'where'. I scan the text and see 'big shiny city'. That matches option C!
